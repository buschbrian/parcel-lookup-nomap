Short-Term Rental Licensing Lookup

Back to the general property lookup

Use an address or parcel number to screen two published licensing-map questions: whether the parcel appears in Millcreek's June 2026 short-term-rental layer, and whether it touches the published 400-foot buffer around another listed property.

This is an initial screen, not a license decision. A clear result does not reserve a license or show compliance with primary-residence, district-cap, parking, inspection, tax, or other requirements. Business Licensing makes the official determination.

Check a property

Type at least 3 characters, then choose an address. Example: 3300 E Santa Rosa Ave.

    Before applying

    Millcreek's current Business Licensing page says a short-term-rental property must be the applicant's primary residence and requires a state sales tax certificate. It also lists a site plan, rental inspection checklist, compliance declaration, and business-name verification when applicable.

    Data source and interpretation

    Results use the Short Term Rental Parcels - June 2026 and Short Term Rental Buffer - June 2026 layers published in Millcreek's public Planning web map. The lookup tests the full parcel polygon. A boundary touch counts as an intersection. When the selected parcel itself appears in the rental layer, its own buffer is excluded from the “another published rental” result.

    The layers are a dated public-map snapshot and may lag a new license, renewal, expiration, revocation, parcel edit, or corrected record. The result is not proof that a property holds a current license and is not approval or denial of an application.

    Accessibility

    If this page prevents you from getting a licensing screen, call Business Licensing at 801-214-2759. Staff can look up the property and provide the result in an accessible format.

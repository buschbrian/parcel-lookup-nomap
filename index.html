Property Lookup

Millcreek city homepage

Enter an address or parcel number to get key zoning, flood hazard, overlay zone, district and service information as text. This lookup does not require a map, a mouse, or vision. For short-term-rental licensing, use the separate business licensing and 400-foot buffer lookup.

Find a property

Type at least 3 characters, then choose from the list of matches. Example: 3300 E Santa Rosa Ave, or the 14‑digit parcel number 16264570030000.

    Data sources and disclaimer

    Parcel, ownership and valuation data originate with the Salt Lake County Recorder and Assessor. Zoning, overlay zone and district data are maintained by Millcreek. Flood data come from FEMA and are compared with Millcreek's published flood layer. Surface fault rupture special-study-area data come from the public Planning map. Address data originate with the Utah Geospatial Resource Center (UGRC) and Salt Lake County.

    This lookup is for general information and screening only. Results may be incomplete or out of date and are not an official determination. Confirm important information with the responsible agency.

    For zoning and development questions, contact Planning & Zoning at 801-214-2700.

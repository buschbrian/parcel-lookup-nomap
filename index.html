Millcreek, Utah

Property Lookup

Enter an address or parcel number to get zoning, flood hazard, overlay zone, district and service information as text. This tool provides the same information as the interactive zoning map and does not require a map, a mouse, or vision.

Find a property

Type at least 3 characters, then choose from the list of matches. Example: 3300 E Santa Rosa Ave, or the 14‑digit parcel number 16264570030000.

    Results

    Prefer to ask a person?

    Staff will look up any property and read or send you the results. This is an equally valid way to get this information.

    • Phone: 801-214-2754
    • Email: gis@millcreekut.gov
    • Response commitment: within 5 business days, or sooner for a permit or hearing deadline.

    Data sources and disclaimer

    Parcel, ownership and valuation data originate with the Salt Lake County Recorder and Assessor. Zoning, overlay zone, hazard and district data are maintained by Millcreek. Address data originate with the Utah Geospatial Resource Center (UGRC) and Salt Lake County.

    This tool reports the data of record. It is not a zoning verification letter, a flood determination for lending or insurance purposes, or a determination that a property can be developed. For a binding determination, contact Planning and Development Services at 801-214-2754.

# Municipal brand asset

`millcreek-logo.png` is the complete Millcreek logo served by the official municipal website:

<https://millcreekut.gov/ImageRepository/Document?documentID=3446>

The image is stored locally so the lookup pages do not depend on a third-party request, content
security policy exception, or continued CivicPlus asset URL at runtime. Do not recolor, redraw, or
replace it without approval from the municipal brand owner.

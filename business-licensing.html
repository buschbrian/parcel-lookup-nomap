Short-Term Rental Licensing Lookup

Millcreek city homepage

Back to the general property lookup

Use an address or parcel number to screen two published licensing-map questions: whether the parcel appears in Millcreek's June 2026 short-term-rental layer, and whether it touches the published 400-foot buffer around another listed property.

This is a screening result, not an official license, eligibility, distance, approval, or denial decision. Confirm with Business Licensing.

Check a property

Type at least 3 characters, then choose an address. Example: 3300 E Santa Rosa Ave.

    Results

    Before applying

    Millcreek's current Business Licensing page says a short-term-rental property must be the applicant's primary residence and requires a state sales tax certificate. It also lists a site plan, rental inspection checklist, compliance declaration, and business-name verification when applicable.

    Review the official requirements and application link before relying on this screen.

    Ask Business Licensing

    For an official buffer or license determination, contact the staff currently listed by Millcreek:

    • Alex Wendt, Business License Administrator: 801-214-2759 or awendt@millcreekut.gov
    • Amy Barber, Deputy Business License Administrator: 801-214-2718 or abarber@millcreekut.gov

    How this screen works

    Results use dated public map layers and may be incomplete or out of date. The lookup compares the selected parcel boundary with mapped short-term-rental parcels and buffers. A boundary touch counts as a map intersection, and the selected parcel's own buffer is not counted as another rental.
